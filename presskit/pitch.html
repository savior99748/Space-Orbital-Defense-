ORBITAL DEFENSE

Tower defense on rotating orbit rings across our Solar System.

Working title · Independent developer · Playable now on web and mobile browsers

The hook

Players build satellites on rings that orbit the planet. Because the rings rotate, defenses sweep across the sky, and timing placement matters as much as choosing it. Moons carry their own rings and pull invaders in with gravity, so players lure enemies into kill zones. It's familiar tower defense with a twist you can understand in one glance.

What's built

  • 9 worlds: all 8 planets with their real moons, plus a Solar System finale where every planet orbits the Sun
  • 7 satellites × 2 specializations each; art evolves at every tier (49 designs)
  • Bosses, 30-wave campaign, endless Freeplay, 3 difficulties
  • Tutorial, stats screen, 10 achievements, auto-save, pinch-zoom
  • Generated sound effects and music

Platforms & tech

  • HTML5 / JavaScript, one codebase: web, installable phone app, iOS + Android (Capacitor), Steam (Electron)
  • Platform layer already handles ads, saves and achievements (CrazyGames SDK integrated)
  • Short sessions (2–20 min), works offline, portrait and landscape

Business model

  • Free-to-play with optional rewarded ads (bonus credits, one revive per game). Hooks already in place
  • Planned: remove-ads purchase, map and satellite packs, cosmetic skins
  • Premium version for Steam
