About the game

Alien invaders are spiraling in toward the planets of our Solar System. Build satellites on rotating orbit rings around each world: because the rings turn, your defenses sweep across the sky, and timing your placements matters as much as choosing them.

Every planet plays differently. Earth's Moon carries its own turret ring and pulls invaders in with its gravity. Saturn's icy rings slow everything that crosses them. Venus spins backwards. In the final stand, all eight planets orbit the Sun at their own speeds while you defend the Sun itself.

  • Rotating orbit rings: defenses sweep around the planet; moons carry rings of their own.
  • 9 worlds: all eight planets with their real moons, plus a full Solar System finale.
  • Gravity: moons and planets bend invader paths, so you can lure enemies into your turrets.
  • 7 satellites, 14 specializations: each satellite branches at tier 3, and its look changes at every tier.
  • Bosses: Motherships launch drones; the Dreadnought ends the 30-wave campaign.
  • Easy / Medium / Hard, endless Freeplay, 10 achievements, a stats screen and a guided tutorial.
  • Plays anywhere: touch, mouse or keyboard, with pinch-zoom on phones and offline play.
