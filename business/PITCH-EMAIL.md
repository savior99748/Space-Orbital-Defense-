# Publisher pitch email template

Keep it short: publishers get many pitches. Replace everything in [brackets]. Attach (or link) one 15-second clip for mobile publishers, or the landscape trailer for web/PC publishers.

---

**Subject:** Pitch: Orbital Defense, a tower defense game on rotating orbit rings (playable now)

Hi [Name / Publishing team],

I'm [your name], an independent developer. I'd like to introduce **Orbital Defense**, a tower defense game where players build satellites on **rings that orbit the planet**. The rings rotate, so defenses sweep across the sky, and moons pull invaders in with gravity. It's familiar tower defense with a twist players understand at a glance.

- **Play it now (browser, phone or desktop):** https://savior99748.github.io/Space-Orbital-Defense-/
- **itch.io page:** https://savior99748.itch.io/orbital-defense
- **15-second clip / trailer:** [link to clip from the press kit]
- **Press kit and one-page pitch:** https://savior99748.github.io/Space-Orbital-Defense-/presskit/

What's built: 9 worlds (all 8 planets with their real moons plus a Solar System finale), 7 satellites with 14 specializations, bosses, 3 difficulties, a tutorial, achievements and auto-save. It's an HTML5 codebase with builds ready for web portals, iOS/Android and Steam, and hooks for rewarded ads.

[Optional, once you have stats: Early numbers: D1 retention X%, tutorial completion Y%, average session Z minutes.]

I'm looking for a publishing partner for [mobile / web / Steam]. I'd love to hear whether it fits your portfolio, and I'm happy to send a build or integrate your SDK for a test.

Thanks for your time,
[Your name]
[Email] · [Phone, optional]

---

**Tips**
- Send to one publisher at a time, or a few, with a personal first line ("I loved how you launched [their game]…").
- Mobile publishers (Kwalee Hitseeker, CrazyLabs) often prefer their own upload forms. Use the 15-second clips there.
- Follow up once after about 10 days. If it's a no, ask what they'd want to see changed.
