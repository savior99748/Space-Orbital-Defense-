Privacy Policy

Orbital Defense · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Orbital Defense is a game. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts or photos, and it has no accounts.

Data stored on your device

The game saves your settings, best scores, achievements and your current game in your browser's or app's local storage on your own device. This data stays on your device. It is not sent to us. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Anonymous gameplay statistics

To improve the game, some versions count anonymous gameplay events, such as "a game started on Earth," "wave 10 reached," "tutorial finished," or "a player returned the next day." These counts help us see which parts are too hard or confusing.

  • No cookies, no advertising IDs, and no personal information are used for these statistics.
  • Events are counted with GoatCounter, a privacy-focused analytics service. Like any website request, it receives basic technical information (such as your IP address and browser type) when an event is sent. See GoatCounter's privacy policy for how it handles that.
  • You can turn statistics off at any time with the 📊 Stats button on the main menu. The game also respects your browser's "Do Not Track" setting.

Ads on game portals and mobile versions

Versions published on game websites (such as CrazyGames) or in app stores may show optional ads, for example to earn bonus credits. Those ads are provided by the platform or ad network, which has its own privacy policy that applies to the ads. The free version on GitHub Pages shows no ads.

Desktop (Steam) version

If you play through Steam, Steam may record achievements and play time under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.

Contact
